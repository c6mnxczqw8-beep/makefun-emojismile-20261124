# One Emoji, One Smile — 🌞

Entry for the make.fun bounty "One Emoji, One Smile". Running
`python3 main.py` writes exactly one emoji — 🌞 (U+1F31E SUN WITH FACE,
1 codepoint, 4 UTF-8 bytes, no trailing newline) — to `/output/entry.txt`
(falling back to `./entry.txt` where `/output` cannot be created).

Why 🌞: a smiling sun is literal warmth plus a smile — warm at first
glance, universal for any age or background, clearly chosen rather than
the default smiley 😊 (the draft-1 pick), and free of the hearts of 🥰,
the hug of 🤗, the tears of 🥹, and any irony or flirtation that could
read oddly from a stranger. The full candidate review (12 candidates,
each verified as a single codepoint) and the refinement pass that
promoted 🌞 over the shortlist are in `CHOICE.md`.
